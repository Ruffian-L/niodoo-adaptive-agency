# Map Language

This project uses its own expedition language.

## Coordinate

A coordinate is a hypothesis joined to what happened when we tested it. Wrong routes stay visible because they locate the terrain. A coordinate is useful even when it is not the destination.

## Milestone

A milestone is a boost, setting, or decision that refueled the climb toward a destination. It records motion: what changed, why it changed, and what became reachable next.

## Flag

A flag marks a destination that was reached. It is not ownership, exclusion, or a wall around the result. It tells later explorers: this place exists, this route reached it, and you can start here or find another way.

## Climb map

The climb map connects coordinates, milestones, and flags. It keeps dead ends, near misses, boosts, and arrivals in one readable trail so later explorers inherit direction rather than only a final answer.

## Double seal

Two seals on one MVP. The **Grok Seal** is the planted hard-route flag. The
**Jason & Sol Seal** is the same MVP confirmed through natural rooms with Nex so the
hard route cannot fall out of the story. They do not replace each other. Their names
are mnemonic handles, not ownership, rank, or exclusive credit. The later 56-memory
durability stress test extends and bounds the two seals; it is not a third seal. See
[SEAL_20260814.md](../seal/SEAL_20260814.md).
